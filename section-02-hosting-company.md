# 2 The hosting company: KPMG

## 2.1 KPMG as a global organisation

KPMG is one of the four large international professional services networks, together with Deloitte, EY, and PwC. It is a global organisation of independent member firms that operate under the KPMG brand and are affiliated with KPMG International Limited. The member firms work in 143 countries and territories and employ more than 275,000 people (KPMG International, 2025). The organisation delivers three lines of service: Audit, Tax and Legal, and Advisory. Audit covers the assurance of financial statements, Tax and Legal covers fiscal and legal advice, and Advisory covers consulting on strategy, operations, risk, transactions, and technology.

## 2.2 KPMG in Italy and the Advisory practice

In Italy, KPMG operates through a set of member entities that cover the three service lines. According to KPMG's own reporting, the Italian organisation has around 5,000 professionals across about 25 offices and serves more than 6,000 clients (KPMG in Italy, 2026). Advisory services in Italy are delivered mainly through KPMG Advisory S.p.A., based in Milan.

The Advisory practice works with organisations on change programmes and on performance improvement. One area of this work is data and analytics: the design and construction of the data platforms, semantic models, and reporting systems that let an organisation use its own operational data for management decisions. The internship described in this thesis sits in this area, on an engagement for a university client.

## 2.3 The Junior Data Engineer role

The author joined KPMG Advisory as a Junior Data Engineer. The internship lasted three months and was extended by a further two, after which it converted into an apprenticeship contract.

A Junior Data Engineer on an Advisory engagement of this kind works on the data side of the delivery. The work covers the pipelines that move data from the source systems into the warehouse, the warehouse tables that the reporting layer consumes, and the semantic models and measures built on top of them. On the San Raffaele engagement, the author's work concentrated on the gold layer of the Snowflake warehouse and on the Power BI semantic model and the DAX measures for the student careers domain, within the framework and the conventions set by the delivery team. Sections 5.3 and 5.6 describe this contribution and its boundary with the work of the team.

## 2.4 The San Raffaele engagement as an example of the practice

The engagement analysed in this thesis, the design of a KPI and dashboard system for Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele, is an example of the data and analytics work of KPMG Advisory. It has the shape these engagements usually take: a client whose operational systems hold the data but not the analytical views that management needs, a delivery team that builds the data platform and the reporting layer, and the handover of a governed reporting product that the client can use without a technical intermediary for each question.

Sections 4 to 6 describe this engagement in detail. This section places it in its organisational context. It is one project within the Advisory practice, staffed by a delivery team, in which the author was the member responsible for the semantic model and the measures of the student careers domain.
